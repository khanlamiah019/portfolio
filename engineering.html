Wage Theft Case Intelligence

Data Visualization · Public Records · Search & Analytics

A searchable intelligence dashboard built for legal advocates and workers, turning messy, publicly available New York State Court records into something a lawyer can actually use. It indexes 3,059 wage-theft cases filed between 2005 and 2026, with filters for industry, county, case status, and violation type, an analytics view charting cases by industry, year, county, and status, and a repeat-offender lookup so advocates can flag employers with a pattern of violations. It will be helpful to Reclamo's 3M+ non-English-speaking users, closing a critical financial literacy gap for vulnerable populations and helping recover owed wages.

StudBuds

React · Spring Boot · Firebase Auth · PostgreSQL

A study-buddy matching app for Cooper Union students, built with a four-person team. Users sign up with a school email, set their availability and subjects they want to learn or teach, and swipe through matches (Tinder-style) with animal avatars standing in for faces until a match is confirmed. Students are matched based on complementary academic strengths, weaknesses, and availability, aiming to accelerate peer learning and strengthen understanding of challenging concepts!

🏆 Pfizer Digital Health Hackathon

CUCU

Mood Classification · Gamified Journaling · Peer Community

A mental health platform for NYC DOE high school students and their school's social workers alike. For students, journaling happens by chatting with Cucu, a pigeon companion, which doubles as a habit loop through CuCoins, streaks, and achievements like "Beginner Journalist" and "Flocking Together," building emotional resilience and self-awareness one check-in at a time. Under the hood, a random forest model paired with sentiment analysis reads each journal entry to detect mood shifts, rolling those check-ins into a Mood Calendar filterable by specific emotion across a weekly view. A Classes feature surfaces anonymized, class-level mood trends for social workers, giving them a school-wide view of student wellbeing so they can reach out to students who may need support sooner, alongside a light peer chat for students themselves.
